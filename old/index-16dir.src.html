The sheet, row by row

  • Most rows are close to their label, but three rows face the wrong way: 247.5° points up and to the right, 270° points straight up, and 315° points up and to the left.
  • Nothing on the sheet faces between about 205° and 330°, the whole lower arc. Measured mode fills it by turning the top-down ant from the 90° row.
  • The eight frames in a row differ only a little, so the walk reads as a shuffle. Stepping frames by distance walked keeps the feet from sliding.
